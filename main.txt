#4. projekt: Intelligens multimodális egészségügyi AI-ügynök
 
## MSc-szakdolgozati cím
**A Multimodal, Explainable AI Decision-Support Prototype for Medical Image and Clinical-Text Analysis**
 
## Kutatási irány
Create a research prototype that combines medical-image analysis with structured clinical context and an LLM explanation layer. The system must be decision support only, not an autonomous diagnostic tool.
 
## Kiinduló projektek
- `starter_ai_agents/ai_medical_imaging_agent`
- `rag_tutorials/vision_rag`
- `rag_tutorials/multimodal_agentic_rag`
 
## MSc-szintű hozzájárulás
Measure image interpretation, retrieval grounding, explanation quality, calibration, and failure cases. Use de-identified or public data, document data-protection and clinical-safety boundaries, and compare image-only with image-plus-text workflows.
 
## Javasolt kutatási kérdés
Can grounded multimodal retrieval improve the usefulness and verifiability of LLM-generated explanations for medical-image decision support?
