The brief

Project #4, as assigned

Thesis title

A Multimodal, Explainable AI Decision-Support Prototype for Medical Image and Clinical-Text Analysis

Suggested research question

Can grounded multimodal retrieval improve the usefulness and verifiability of LLM-generated explanations for medical-image decision support?

What it requires

  • Decision support only, never an autonomous diagnosis
  • Public or de-identified data only
  • Measure interpretation, grounding, explanation quality, calibration and failures
  • Compare image-only with image-plus-text workflows

Three starter projects

ai_medical_imaging_agent · vision_rag · multimodal_agentic_rag

The brief comes from main.txt. Two constraints shape everything: decision support only, and public or de-identified data. The three starters are tutorial apps from awesome-llm-apps, analysed in codebase_analysis.md.

The semester project

Three questions, one built system

Q1 · Fitness

How do the three starters work, and how well do they detect chest-X-ray findings with minimal adaptation?

Q2 · Strengths

Which of their parts are worth carrying into a grounded image + text system, and which gaps must be filled?

Q3 · Comparison

How much do the built system’s results differ from the starters, from a specialist image model and from retrieval alone?

Left for the thesis: the full ladder, mechanism probes, calibration analysis, claim-level checks, human rating, MIMIC-CXR and local models.

The semester plan (semester_project/semester_plan.md) is the thesis’s pilot study and first chapter. It is frozen: any change is recorded as a deviation in results.md and the diary.

The semester project

How the comparison is scored

Two scoring paths

Against the starters

Every system’s free text goes through the same CheXbert labeller (for G, its impression). Labeller accuracy is checked per system.

Against S0 and R0

G’s JSON scores are used directly, alongside their continuous scores.

MetricSystems
F1, sensitivity, specificityAll
AUROCS0, R0, G
Retrieval precision@kP2, P3, R0, G
Copying rateG vs. R0
Citation agreementG
Effect of clinical textG vs. G−ind

Paired bootstrap confidence intervals. The comparison shows how much results differ, not which change caused it.

Attribution of differences to single design choices is the thesis ladder’s job. The copying rate checks whether G merely repeats its neighbours’ labels.

Done so far

What the data checks found

No patient IDs, yet no leakage risk

The release has only study IDs, but the dataset paper states: “In no case did we take more than one study per patient.” A study split is a patient split.

No frontal/lateral labels

Views classified zero-shot with BiomedCLIP, checked by eye: 60/60 random correct; all 11 low-score picks frontal; 40/40 exclusions lateral.

Clean images

4 unusable images dropped (3 blank, 1 noise). No duplicates by embedding or pixel checks.

S0 weights checked

TorchXRayVision’s combined weights were trained on IU X-Ray (Open-i), so they are ruled out; weights without it remain.

Source for the one-study-per-patient fact: Demner-Fushman et al., JAMIA 2016;23(2):304–310. The paper’s public-subset counts (3,955 reports, 7,470 images) match the downloaded files exactly.

Done so far

Ground truth: a labeller error, and a fix

CheXbert labelled 55 of 62 reports reading “…Heart and mediastinum normal.” as Cardiomegaly: 13% of all its Cardiomegaly positives. Removing the de-identification masks did not help.

Decision

Ground truth = the dataset’s manual MeSH codes. CheXbert stays as the labeller for system outputs, so its error is measured directly.

FindingPositivesCheXbert κ
Lung Opacity5190.73
Cardiomegaly2950.87

The six findings were chosen by count and because S0 can score all of them (Support Devices dropped). Positives are among the 3,604 ground-truth studies. κ is CheXbert’s agreement with the manual codes.

Models retired

  • gemini-2.5-pro (P1) and gemini-2.5-flash (P2): no longer available to new users
  • Both now run on gemini-3-flash-preview, as P1-adapted and P2-adapted
  • P2 embeds with Gemini Embedding 2 instead of Cohere

Free tier

20

requests per day per model: about 190 days for the ~3,850 requests planned, so paid quota is needed

Runners built

  • P2-adapted: smoke test OK (normal case answered correctly)
  • P1-adapted: two bugs fixed (hidden API errors; no request timeout)
  • Quota-aware stopping and a request budget; 9/9 offline tests pass
The free-tier limit was measured from the API’s own error (GenerateRequestsPerDayPerProjectPerModel-FreeTier, limit 20). The 503 errors seen earlier were Google-side overload, not the quota.
